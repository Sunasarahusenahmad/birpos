# BirStock Brand Kit

All files are generated by `python scripts/brand/generate.py` from vector masters
(the mark and the wordmark are drawn as paths - no fonts required). Re-run it after
changing prices or `scripts/brand/og-pages.json`.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| BirStock Orange | `#ED6211` | Primary brand colour, buttons, accents |
| Orange Light → Dark gradient | `#F7883A` → `#C94A06` | The "B" mark |
| Ink | `#18181B` | Wordmark, text, dark backgrounds |
| Ink Soft | `#1F1F24` | App icon background |
| Cream | `#FFFAF3` | Light backgrounds |

Fonts: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (wordmark & headings), **Inter** (body).
Wordmark: "bir" Medium + "stock" ExtraBold, lowercase.

## Folders
- `logo/` - full logo (colour, white, mono black/white, orange), logo on white/cream/dark/orange
  backgrounds (PNG + JPG), stacked logo, standalone mark and app icons. Every variant has an SVG.
  - Website / documents on light backgrounds: `birstock-logo.svg`
  - Dark backgrounds: `birstock-logo-white.svg`
  - Transparent PNGs: `birstock-logo.png` (1200px) and `@2x` (2400px)
- `favicon/` - favicon.ico (16/32/48), PNG favicons, SVG icon, Apple touch icon, Android
  & maskable icons, Safari pinned tab, Windows tile.
- `social/` - ready-to-upload images:
  - `profile/` - profile pictures (orange, dark, white) at 1080 & 400
  - `instagram/` - profile 320, intro post 1080x1080, story 1080x1920
  - `facebook/` - profile 720, cover 1640x624 (key content inside the mobile-safe centre), post, story
  - `linkedin/` - logo 400, company cover 1128x191 (+2x), post
  - `x-twitter/` - header 1500x500
  - `youtube/` - profile 800, banner 2560x1440 (content inside the 1546x423 safe area)
  - `whatsapp/` - business profile 640
  - `email/` - email signature logo 600x160 (+2x)
- `og/` - Open Graph / social share images (1200x630) for every page of the website.

## Usage rules
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "B" stroke × 4.
- Don't recolour the wordmark outside the provided variants, stretch, or add effects.
- On photos or busy backgrounds use `birstock-logo-on-dark` or `birstock-logo-on-white`.
