# BirStock Product Launch Video: Complete Production Prompt

> **How to use this file:** give this whole file to the video team, editor, or AI video tool.
> **Rule #1 for whoever runs it:** do **not** start producing yet. First ask every question in **Section 1**, wait for the answers, and confirm the plan back in one short summary. Only then start.

---

## 0. The goal in one line

> **Recording scope:** the team records **only the BirStock desktop app** (Windows). The **Mobile App is never screen-recorded.** Every mobile shot is built from the provided mobile **screenshots** (see Section 6.2).

Make a **product launch film** for **BirStock**, in the style of a new smartphone launch (Apple / OnePlus / Nothing): premium, cinematic, beat-synced and confident. It must tell one story:

> **"Most billing software makes you rent it and keeps your data on their cloud. BirStock is billing software your shop owns."**

The product is the hero. Show **real BirStock screens**, beautifully, the way a photographer shows their best photos.

## 4. Creative style (the "phone launch" look)

### 4.1 Overall feel
- Opens like a smartphone launch: **dark stage, a single light sweep, the product floats in.**
- **3D device mockups:** a laptop/monitor playing the desktop screen recording, plus an Android phone showing the Mobile App **screenshots**. They rotate slowly (5–15°), float, and cast soft shadows.
- **Macro "camera" moves into the UI:** slow push-ins, parallax, rack-focus between desktop and phone.
- **Kinetic typography:** short 2–5 word lines, one at a time, snapping to the music beat.
- **Pace:** 1.5–2.5 seconds per shot in the demo section. Hold the hero shots for 3–4 seconds.

### 4.2 Paper-cut effect (for the PROBLEM section and transitions)
- The "problem" scenes (internet down, renewal bill, data on someone else's server) are built in a **layered paper-cut style**: cut-out paper layers in cream/paper/orange tones, visible paper texture, soft drop shadows between layers, slight depth parallax.
- Animate at a **stop-motion feel (12 fps "on twos")** for the paper scenes only. Everything else runs at smooth 60 fps.
- Props as paper cut-outs: a Wi-Fi icon tearing apart, a renewal invoice growing taller and taller, a cloud with a padlock, a paper Khata notebook.
- **Transition from problem to solution:** a **paper tear / rip reveal**. The paper world tears open and the clean real BirStock screen is revealed underneath. This is the key "reveal" moment.
- Small paper-fold transitions (page flip / fold-away) can be reused between demo chapters. Use them no more than 3 times.

### 4.3 Click & interaction effects (for the screen demo)
- **Cursor:** enlarged (1.5×), smooth-eased, with a soft shadow. No jittery mouse movement.
- **Click ripple:** an orange `#ED6211` ring that expands and fades out (250 ms) on every click, with a soft click SFX.
- **Key-cap overlay:** when a keyboard shortcut is used, show a 3D key-cap popup (e.g. **F2**, **F10**, **Ctrl+P**) in the bottom-centre, white key with orange outline, plus a key-press SFX.
- **Barcode scan:** a red laser line sweeps, the item row flashes orange tint `#FFF3EB`, and a scanner **beep** plays.
- **Number roll:** totals (₹1,020.00, ₹815.55) count up quickly while GST recalculates.
- **Spotlight/zoom:** dim the rest of the screen to 40% and zoom 1.3–1.8× into the area being explained (cart total, Khata balance, low-stock badge).
- **Success moment:** on "Save & Print", a thermal receipt slides out of the bottom of the frame (3D paper) with a printer SFX.

### 4.4 Text style on screen
- Max **5 words per line**, max 2 lines on screen.
- Headlines: Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, ink on cream or white on ink, with one key word in orange.
- Keep all text inside **safe zones** (9:16: avoid top 220 px and bottom 380 px for Reels UI).

## 6. Screen recording spec

### 6.1 Desktop app: record live (the only thing we record)

**Setup**
- Windows display scaling **100%**, resolution **1920×1200** (the app's native shots) or **2560×1600**. Record at **60 fps** with OBS (CQP/CRF 16–18, MKV, then remux to MP4).
- Hide the taskbar, turn on Focus Assist (no notifications), close other apps, use a clean wallpaper.
- Use the **"Sample Mart" demo database only** (Palanpur sample data). Check that no real customer names or phone numbers appear.
- The cursor moves slowly and deliberately, pauses 0.5 s before each click, and never wiggles. Record **3 takes** of every action.
- Record **without** cursor highlight. Click ripples and key-caps are added in post.
- Record each action as a **separate clip**, named as in Section 9.

**Shot list to record**
1. Quick POS: scan 4 items (Basmati Rice 5 kg, Toor Dal 1 kg, Sunflower Oil 1 L, Bath Soap pack of 4). Total ₹1,020.00. **Ctrl+P** Save & Print.
2. Quick POS: item discount (F3) and bill discount (F9), with GST recalculating.
3. Touch/Grid POS: tap 2 products, pick UPI, **F10** Save.
4. Hold Cart: open bill tabs #1 → #2 → #3 and switch between them.
5. **Offline test:** with an **activated** licence, switch Wi-Fi OFF on camera, create and save a bill. **First test that this works on the current build.** If licence status blocks it, skip this shot and tell us.
6. Thermal print preview and A4 GST invoice preview. **Do NOT open the paper-size dropdown (A5 option).**
7. Customer Khata: Ramesh Patel ledger → Add Payment → Download Statement.
8. Dashboard: open the app, cards load. Click "Low / Out of stock: 2 Products".
9. Reports: change the period, chart updates, Download PDF.
10. Inventory: Excel import of the product list.
11. Settings → Mobile Access QR (desktop side only). **IP address and pairing code must be blurred in post.**
12. Backup: Google Drive backup run (use a demo Google account, and blur the email address).

**Still-image fallback:** if a desktop shot can't be recorded, animate it from the real desktop screenshots (pan/zoom, masked UI elements sliding in). Use the list in Section 8.

### 6.2 Mobile App: do NOT record, build from screenshots

The Mobile App is shown **only** using the provided screenshots (720×1608, demo data, clean status bar). Animate them so they feel live:

- Place each screenshot inside a **3D Android phone mockup** (thin bezel, ink `#111827` frame, soft shadow). The phone floats and rotates slightly.
- **Fake the interaction:** an orange tap ripple on the button being "pressed", then a quick **slide or crossfade** to the next screenshot (200–300 ms).
- **Scroll** long screens by panning the screenshot inside the phone's screen mask.
- **Spotlight/zoom** into one area (e.g. today's sales ₹3,346.10, the "Charge ₹840.00" button, the low-stock alert).
- **Don't add or change UI** that isn't in the screenshot. No made-up buttons, numbers or screens.
- Phone sequence to use: `app-pair-scan` → `app-home` → `app-new-bill` → `app-payment-change` → `app-bill-done`. Optional extras: `app-stock`, `app-customers`, `app-invoices`.
- If a needed mobile screen is missing, **ask us for a new screenshot**. Don't record the phone.
- Always mention "Mobile App included in Pro & Lifetime" when the phone appears.

## 7. Audio spec

### Music
- Mood: **modern, premium, optimistic tech launch with a subtle Indian touch** (a light tabla/percussion layer or a short santoor/flute motif in the intro and end). Not Bollywood, not corporate-ukulele.
- 105–120 BPM. Needs a **clear riser + drop** near 0:12 and a clean ending (no fade-out, it should end on a hit).
- References (style only, do not copy): Apple product-launch films, OnePlus/Nothing launch teasers.
- Cut every demo shot **on the beat**.
- License must cover Instagram, YouTube, Meta/Google ads and the website, worldwide, forever.

### Voiceover
- Record at 48 kHz / 24-bit, dry room, no reverb. Read the script in Section 5 at a natural speed (about 150 wpm).
- Deliver: raw takes plus a cleaned, de-essed, compressed final.

### Sound effects (layer subtly, never louder than the VO)
| SFX | Where |
|---|---|
| Paper rip / tear (big) | Problem → reveal at 0:12 |
| Paper rustle, fold, flip | Paper-cut scenes, chapter transitions |
| Soft UI click | Every click ripple |
| Keyboard key press | Every key-cap overlay (Ctrl+P, F10, F3, F9) |
| Barcode scanner beep | Each scan |
| Thermal printer zip | Receipt slide-out |
| Number tick | Totals counting up |
| Whoosh / swoosh | Camera moves, logo, price cards |
| Wi-Fi off blip | Offline moment |
| Phone tap + success chime | Mobile app bill |
| Cash register "ka-ching" | Lifetime price card |
| Impact hit | Music drop, final logo |

### Mix
- Final loudness **-14 LUFS integrated**, true peak **-1 dBTP**.
- Music ducks **-12 to -16 dB** under the VO. SFX sit **-6 to -10 dB** below the VO.
- Also deliver a **music + SFX only** version (no VO) for the silent website loop and for other languages.
